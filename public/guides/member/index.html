Part 1

Getting started

Signing in, activating your account and using the portal on your phone.

Getting started · 1

Sign in

Open the member portal sign-in page on your phone or computer. BWF gives every member a username and a temporary password for the first sign-in.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Email or username

    The first time, type the username BWF gave you (for example BWFCC101). After you activate your account you can use your own username or your email.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Password

    Type the temporary password from BWF the first time. After activation, use the private password you chose.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Keep me signed in

    Leave this ticked on your own phone so you don't have to sign in every time. Untick it on a shared or office computer.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Sign in

    Opens the portal. If you are signing in for the first time you will be taken to the activation screen first.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Forgot password?

    Sends a reset code to your registered email so you can set a new password.

Getting started · 2

First-time activation

The first time you sign in with the temporary password, the portal asks you to make the account your own. You only do this once.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Your temporary username

    Shows the username BWF gave you, so you know you're on the right account.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Choose your own username

    Pick a username you will remember: 4 to 30 letters or numbers (dots, dashes and underscores allowed). It replaces the temporary one.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Email and verification code

    Type your email and tap Send code. BWF emails you a 6-digit code; type it in the Verification code box.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: New private password

    At least 12 characters, and not the temporary password. Type it twice.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Activate & continue

    Finishes activation and opens your dashboard.

Good to know
  • From now on, sign in with your new username or email and your new password. The temporary password stops working.
  • Keep your password private. BWF staff can reset it but can never see it.
Getting started · 3

Using the portal on your phone

The portal works in your phone's browser. No app download is needed.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Menu button

    Tap ☰ to open the full menu. Tap a section to go there; the menu closes by itself.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Your score

    Same dashboard as on a computer, arranged for a narrow screen. Scroll down for everything else.

Good to know
  • Tip: in your phone browser choose 'Add to Home screen' to open the portal like an app.
Getting started · 4

The phone menu

The menu groups every section the same way as on a computer.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Overview

    Dashboard, Meetings, Payments and My Points.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Record activity

    Where you log referrals, thank you slips, one-to-ones, power dates, conclaves, consumers and chief guests.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Insights & network

    Your activity reports and the nearby-member search. Scroll the menu for My Profile, Articles and Sign out.

Part 2

Overview

Your dashboard, meetings, meeting check-in, payments and points.

Overview · 5

Dashboard

The dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It shows your score, quick buttons for the things you do most, this month's numbers and your next meeting.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Menu

    Every section of the portal. On a phone, tap the ☰ button at the top right to open this menu.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Overall BWF Score

    Your total points from everything you have recorded. Tap it to see how the points add up.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Quick actions

    One tap to record a referral, a thank you slip, a one-to-one or a power date.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Shortcuts

    Jump to your meetings, points, activity reports or the nearby-member search.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: This month

    Referrals given and received, business given and received, one-to-ones and power dates for the current month. 'Full report' opens My Activity.

  6. 6
    Marker 6: Next chapter meeting

    Date, time, venue and chief guest of your chapter's next meeting.

  7. 7
    Marker 7: Your profile

    Link to your public profile page and buttons to edit your profile or submit an article.

  8. 8
    Marker 8: Account & security

    Lost your phone, or signed in on someone else's computer? 'Sign out of all devices' signs you out everywhere.

Overview · 6

Meetings

See your chapter's upcoming meetings. All times are Indian Standard Time (IST).

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Next meeting

    Highlighted in green. Shows the date, time, venue, chief guest and agenda.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Later meetings

    Further upcoming meetings for your chapter.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Recent meetings

    Meetings that have already happened.

Overview · 7

Check in at a meeting (scan the QR)

At the meeting venue, BWF shows a QR code. Scan it with your phone camera. It opens this check-in page in the portal (sign in if asked).

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Which meeting

    The meeting name, chapter, date and time, so you know you scanned the right QR.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Your details

    Your name, category and chapter as BWF has them.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Payment status

    If this month's meeting fee is already approved, the page says so and doesn't ask for payment. Otherwise it asks whether you paid, and you can upload the payment screenshot right here.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Check In

    Tap to mark yourself Present for the meeting.

Good to know
  • Check-in works only while the admin has registration open for that meeting. If it says check-in isn't open, ask your chapter admin.
  • Already checked in? Scanning again won't create a duplicate.
Overview · 8

Payments

Pay your monthly meeting fee or your annual membership, then upload the payment proof so BWF can approve it.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Your status

    Whether this month's meeting fee is paid, the month you are paid up to, and whether your yearly membership is paid.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Choose what you are paying

    Monthly meeting fee or annual membership. The payment details and form change to match.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: How to pay

    Scan the QR code with any UPI app (tap it to enlarge, or Download QR), or transfer directly to the bank account shown.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Submit your payment

    Choose how many months you paid for and which months, type the total amount, the date you paid, and upload the screenshot or receipt. Then tap Submit payment.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Payment history

    Every payment you have submitted with its status: Pending approval, Approved, Rejected or Clarification requested.

Good to know
  • A payment counts only after BWF approves it. Approved months are not asked for again at meeting check-in.
  • Bank details and the QR are blurred in this guide. You will see the real ones in your portal.
Overview · 9

My Points

Every activity you record earns points. This page shows your overall BWF Score and exactly where it comes from.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Overall BWF Score

    The total of all your points.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Activity-wise points

    For each activity: how many you recorded (Count), points for each one, and the subtotal. BWF sets the point values and may change them.

Part 3

Record activity

Log what you do for the network. Every entry counts towards your BWF Score, and the other member sees it too. On each page the form is on the left and your history is on the right.

Record activity · 10

Referrals — share banners

A referral is a genuine business opportunity you pass to another BWF member. 'Outside' means someone from your own network; 'Self / Inside' means you bought from the member yourself.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Share on WhatsApp

    A ready-made banner for each referral you give or receive. Take a screenshot of a banner and post it in your chapter WhatsApp group.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Show all banners

    Only the latest banners show at first. Tap to see the rest.

Record activity · 11

Referrals — record a referral

Use the form at the bottom of the Referrals page each time you pass business to a member.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: 1. Chapter

    Pick the chapter of the member you are referring.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: 2. Category

    Pick their business category. Each chapter has only one member per category.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: 3. Member

    Pick the member, or type part of their name or company to find them.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Type of referral

    Outside = someone from your own network needs their service. Self / inside = you are buying from them yourself.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Description

    Optional. A line about the opportunity, such as who needs what.

  6. 6
    Marker 6: Record Referral

    Saves it. The member sees it as a referral received, and you earn referral points.

  7. 7
    Marker 7: Given and Received

    Lists of the referrals you have passed and the ones passed to you.

Record activity · 12

Thank You Slips

A thank you slip records business that actually happened because of a BWF referral. Referral = opportunity given. Thank you slip = business done.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Business totals

    Business you received: the value of slips you recorded. Business you generated: the value of slips other members recorded to thank you.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Add a Thank You Slip

    Pick the member you are thanking (chapter, category, member), type the business value in ₹, optionally link it to the referral they gave you, add a description and tap Record Thank You Slip.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Given and Received

    Slips you have given and slips you have received, with amounts and dates.

Good to know
  • Each slip also gets a shareable banner at the top of the page, like referrals.
Record activity · 13

One-to-Ones

A one-to-one is a meeting between you and one other member, outside the regular chapter meeting, to understand each other's business. Record it once and it counts for both of you.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Record a One-to-One

    Pick the member you met (chapter, category, member), add notes if you like, then tap Record One-to-One.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Your One-to-Ones

    Everyone you have met one-to-one, with your notes and the date.

Record activity · 14

Power Dates

A power date is when you take a fellow member to meet one of your own business contacts, opening a door for them.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Add a Power Date

    Pick the member you took along, type your contact's name and company, add notes and tap Record Power Date. Took two members? Record one power date for each.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: You hosted

    Power dates you hosted. 'You joined' below lists the ones other members took you on.

Record activity · 15

Conclaves

A conclave is three or more members meeting together outside the regular chapter meeting.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Record a Conclave

    Add at least 2 fellow members (pick each one through chapter and category), add the location and notes, then tap Record Conclave.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Your Conclaves

    Conclaves you organised or joined, with who was there.

Record activity · 16

Consumers

A consumer is someone with a real construction requirement (not a prospective member) that you bring to a chapter meeting.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Record a Consumer

    Type the consumer's name, and optionally company, phone and what they need. Tap Record Consumer.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Consumers brought

    Everyone you have brought, with date.

Record activity · 17

Chief Guests

A chief guest is a business leader or decision-maker you invited to a chapter meeting.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Record a Chief Guest

    Type the guest's name, and optionally designation, company and notes. Tap Record Chief Guest.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Chief Guests brought

    Every chief guest you have brought.

Part 4

Insights & network

Reports on your activity, and a search for members near you.

Insights & network · 18

My Activity

A summary of your BWF activity this week and over a longer period.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: This week

    121s (one-to-ones), RFLs (referrals), BIZ (business), visitors, inductions, chief guests, power dates and consumers for the current week.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Choose the period

    Switch the table between the last 6 months, the last 12 months or your whole membership.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Performance report

    Totals for each activity over the period you chose.

Insights & network · 19

Detailed Reports

The full list behind every number: who, what and when.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Report tabs

    Pick what to see: referrals given or received, business, one-to-ones, power dates, conclaves, visitors, chief guests, consumers or inductions. Scroll sideways on a phone to see all tabs.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Details

    Each entry with the other member, type, description and date.

Insights & network · 20

Find Members Nearby

In an area with time to spare? Find BWF members close by so you can drop in and meet.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Where are you?

    Type an area or locality, for example Royapettah.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Within

    How far to search (5 km by default). Results are sorted nearest first.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Name or company

    Optional. Search for a particular person or company.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Chapter and category

    Optional filters, for example only architects.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Search

    Shows matching members with their business and contact details below.

Part 5

You

Your profile, articles and signing out.

You · 21

My Profile

Keep your public business profile up to date. Changes don't go live straight away: BWF reviews every change first.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Personal

    Name, designation, a short bio and your photo (paste a link or tap Upload file).

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Business profile

    Services, specialisations, what makes you different (USP), years in business, areas served, certifications, major projects and clients.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Contact

    Email, phone, WhatsApp, website, address and Google Maps link. Your business location is used by Find Members Nearby.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Social & media

    Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook and YouTube links, a brochure PDF and a video (upload a file or paste a Google Drive link).

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Submit for review

    Sends your changes to BWF. Your public profile updates once they approve them.

You · 22

Articles

Write an article for the BWF website blog. Nothing is published until an admin approves it.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Title

    The headline of your article.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Excerpt

    Optional one or two line summary shown on the blog list.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Content

    The article itself. You can use simple formatting: # for headings, **bold**, - for bullet points.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Category and image

    Pick a blog category and optionally add a cover image.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Submit for review

    Sends it to BWF. You will see it on the website once it's approved.

You · 23

Signing out

Your name and chapter are always shown at the bottom of the menu.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Your account

    Your name, category and chapter, with the Sign out button below them.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Sign out of all devices

    Use this if you lose your phone or forgot to sign out somewhere. You will need your password again on every device.

Help

Common questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most. Each one links to the page that shows it.

How do I pay my meeting fee or annual membership?

Open Payments, choose Meeting payment (monthly) or Annual membership, pay by UPI QR or bank transfer, then fill in Submit your payment and upload the screenshot. See: Payments →

Where do I find the payment QR code or bank details?

On the Payments page, under How to pay. Tap the QR to enlarge it or use Download QR. See: Payments →

How do I give a referral?

Open Referrals and use Give a Referral at the bottom: pick chapter, category and member, choose Outside or Self, then Record Referral. See: Referrals — record a referral →

Where can I see my points?

My Points shows your Overall BWF Score and the points for each activity. See: My Points →

How do I find BWF members near me?

Open Find Members Nearby, type your area, choose the distance and tap Search. See: Find Members Nearby →

How do I change my company name, chapter or category?

These are set by BWF and aren't on your profile form. Ask your chapter admin to change them. See: My Profile →

Check-in says it isn't open. Why can't I check in?

You can check in only while the admin has registration open for that meeting, and only inside the check-in time window. Ask your chapter admin to open it. See: Check in at a meeting (scan the QR) →

I scanned the QR code but nothing happened.

The QR opens the check-in page in your browser. Sign in if asked, then tap Check In. If it says check-in isn't open, ask your chapter admin. See: Check in at a meeting (scan the QR) →

My payment still shows Pending approval.

BWF checks every payment against the bank before approving it, so it can take a little time. It counts only once it shows Approved. See: Payments →

My payment was rejected or BWF asked for clarification.

Look at the status in Your payment history, check with BWF what was wrong, then submit the correct amount, months and proof again. See: Payments →

Why am I asked to pay the meeting fee again at check-in?

The check-in page asks only when the month isn't approved yet. If you paid but it is still pending, answer No so you don't pay twice. See: Check in at a meeting (scan the QR) →

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in page tap Forgot password? A reset code is sent to your registered email. See: Sign in →

My temporary password doesn't work.

The temporary password stops working once you activate your account. Use the username and password you chose. If you never activated, ask your admin to reset your temporary password. See: First-time activation →

I didn't get the email verification code.

Check your spam folder and tap Send code again. Make sure the email address is typed correctly. See: First-time activation →

I updated my profile but the website hasn't changed.

Profile changes are reviewed by BWF before they go live. Your public profile updates once they are approved. See: My Profile →

My article isn't showing on the website.

Articles are published only after an admin approves them. See: Articles →

I can't find the member I want to refer.

Pick their chapter first, then their category, then the member. You can also type part of their name or company. See: Referrals — record a referral →

What is the difference between a referral and a thank you slip?

A referral is an opportunity you pass to a member. A thank you slip records business that actually happened because of a referral, with its value. See: Thank You Slips →

Why didn't my score go up?

Each activity earns the points BWF sets for it. My Points shows the count and points for every activity, and some activities may be set to 0 points. See: My Points →

I lost my phone or stayed signed in on another computer.

On the dashboard, use Sign out of all devices under Account & security. See: Signing out →

Is there an app to download?

No download is needed. The portal works in your phone's browser; use Add to Home screen to open it like an app. See: Using the portal on your phone →

All screenshots in this guide use demo data. The members, companies, phone numbers and amounts are made up; your portal will show your real chapter. Bank details and payment QR codes are blurred. Questions about your account? Contact your chapter admin.
