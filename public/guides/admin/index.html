Who sees what

What each admin can open depends on their role. The Super Admin can change any of this on the Roles & Permissions page. These are the default settings.

RoleWorkspaces and access
Super AdminEverything, including admin users, roles and permissions, settings, feedback and approving payments.
Central AdminAll four workspaces. Can view payments but not approve them. No access to admin users, roles or feedback.
Website AdminWebsite Admin workspace: members, applications, chapters, categories, companies, meetings, visitors and website content.
Membership AdminMember Performance workspace: roster sheets, QR codes, attendance, invitations, points and BWF App activity.
Marketing AdminMarketing workspace only.
Accounts DepartmentAccounts workspace and payment pages. Can approve, reject and ask for clarification on payments.
Chapter AdminOnly their own chapter, with whichever permissions the Super Admin ticks for the role.
Part 1

Getting started

Signing in, choosing a workspace and finding your way around.

Getting started · 1

Sign in

Admins sign in on the admin sign-in page with the email and password the Super Admin created for them.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Email or username

    Your admin email address.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Password

    Your admin password. After 5 wrong attempts the account locks for 15 minutes.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Sign in

    Opens the workspace chooser.

Getting started · 2

Choose a workspace

The admin panel is split into four workspaces so each team sees only the tools it needs. You only see the workspaces your role allows.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Website Admin

    Members, applications, chapters, categories, companies, member logins, meetings, visitors, website content, blog, testimonials, chief guests, FAQs, feedback, admin users and roles.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Member Performance Admin

    Roster sheets, meeting QR codes, attendance, meeting invitations, visitor QR and attendance, payments, points and the BWF App activity of members.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Marketing Admin

    Social media content calendar, content creation, scheduling, AI script writing, content library and publishing history.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Accounts

    The full payment ledger across all chapters: who paid, for what, pending approvals, approve or reject, and Excel export.

Good to know
  • You can switch workspace at any time from the bottom of the sidebar.
Getting started · 3

Sidebar and your account

Every admin page has the same sidebar. The links in it change with the workspace you picked.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Current workspace

    The name under the logo shows which workspace you are in.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Menu

    The pages in this workspace, grouped by topic. The sidebar scrolls if the list is long.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Switch workspace

    Go back to the workspace chooser.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Change password

    Change your own admin password.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Sign out

    Always sign out on a shared computer.

Part 2

Website Admin

The membership records and the public website: members, applications, chapters, categories, companies, member logins, meetings, visitors, website content and admin accounts.

Website Admin · 4

Dashboard

A snapshot of the whole organisation across all chapters.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Pending approvals

    Membership applications waiting for action. Tap to open them.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Quick links

    Jump straight to Members, Applications, Meetings or Visitors.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Current state

    Active members, companies and chapters, open category slots, pending approvals, upcoming meetings and published blogs.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Period figures

    Pick This Month or a date range, then Apply, to see new visitor registrations, applications, new members and conversions for that period.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Recent admin activity

    The latest actions taken by admins. 'Full activity log' opens the complete history.

Website Admin · 5

Members — list

Every member across all chapters. A chapter can have only one active member per category; the system blocks a second one.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Filter

    Show one chapter or one category, then tap Filter.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Member list

    Name, company, chapter, category and status of each member.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Edit / Deactivate

    Edit opens the member's full profile. Deactivate takes them out of active membership and frees their category in that chapter.

Website Admin · 6

Members — add a member

Scroll to the bottom of the Members page to add a member directly (for example an existing member being moved into the system).

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Add member form

    Name, designation, company (pick an existing one or create a new one), chapter and category, plus contact details. Save to create the member.

Website Admin · 7

Members — edit a member

Open any member from the list to edit their profile, manage their portal login and record who inducted them.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Portal login

    The member's username and login status. 'Revoke access' stops them signing in to the member portal.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Profile sections

    Personal, Business profile, Contact, Social & media and Gallery. Changes here go live immediately when you tap Save profile; member-submitted changes come for review separately.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: View public profile

    Opens the member's page on the public website.

Website Admin · 8

Members — induction and testimonials

Further down the same page.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Save profile

    Saves all profile changes above.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Induction

    If another member invited this person to join, pick them under Referred by and Save. That member gets the induction points.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Testimonials

    Add a testimonial for this member. Tick the consent box to confirm the person agreed; it publishes immediately.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Visitors invited

    Visitors this member has brought to meetings.

Website Admin · 9

Applications

Membership applications from the website's Apply page.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Application list

    Name, category, chapter, status and date submitted. The coloured badge shows where each application is: New, Under review, Contacted, Meeting scheduled, Approved in principle, Waiting for payment, Paid, Rejected or Waitlisted.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: View

    Opens the application.

Website Admin · 10

Application — review and convert

Review an application, keep notes, move it through the stages and, once payment is confirmed, turn it into a member.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Application details

    Everything the applicant filled in on the Apply form.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Notes

    Private notes for the team: calls made, meeting outcomes, follow-ups. Tap Save notes.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Status

    Move the application to its next stage. The status shows on the Applications list.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Convert to member

    Only after the membership payment is confirmed. 'Create member account' creates the real, public member profile from this application.

Website Admin · 11

Chapters

All chapters with their status and member count.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Chapter list

    Active chapters appear on the website. Coming soon chapters show as 'Launching soon'. Draft chapters are internal only.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Edit

    Opens the chapter's settings, leadership and coordinators.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Add a chapter

    Type a name and location. New chapters start as Draft so you can set them up before going public.

Website Admin · 12

Chapter settings

Each chapter's details, meeting schedule and team.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Chapter details

    Name, status, location and the regular meeting schedule: day, which weeks of the month, start time, venue and address. 'Create Next Month's Meetings' uses this schedule.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Founding members

    Members shown as the chapter's founding team.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Leadership

    Assign President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer and other roles. They appear on the chapter page and the roster sheet.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Coordinators

    Meeting duty coordinators (Visitor, Attendance, Hot Seat and more) printed on the roster sheet.

Website Admin · 13

Categories

Business categories. Only one active member per category is allowed in each chapter.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Category list

    How many members hold each category, and whether it is Active, shown on the Apply form and shown in the roster's Open Categories.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Actions

    Hide a category from the Apply form or the roster, or deactivate it. Deactivating doesn't affect existing members.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Add a category

    Type a name and optional description, then tap Add category.

Website Admin · 14

Companies

Companies are separate from members: one company can have several BWF members across chapters.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Company list

    Company name, website, GST number and how many members represent it.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Add a company

    Name, website, GST number, description and logo. Companies can also be created from the Add member form.

Website Admin · 15

Member Login Credentials

Give members their portal logins in bulk. Each member gets a username (BWFCC + chapter number + serial, e.g. BWFCC101) and a one-time temporary password.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Login status counts

    No login: members who have never had credentials. Temporary — not activated: issued but the member hasn't signed in yet. Activated: the member has set their own password.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Generate credentials

    Creates logins for every member who doesn't have one.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Reset to temporary password

    Tick members in the list below first. Gives them a new temporary password. Only works for members who haven't activated yet.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Reset ALL members

    Emergency use only. Gives every active member a new temporary password and signs everyone out. Tell members before you do this.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Filter the list

    Show all members or only one login status.

Website Admin · 16

Member Login Credentials — after generating

After you generate, the temporary passwords appear once. Download the sheet straight away: passwords can't be shown again, only reset.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: New credentials

    Each member's username and temporary password.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Download

    Save as PDF or Excel (CSV). Share each row only with that member, privately.

Website Admin · 17

Meetings

Chapter meetings. Visitors can register online for upcoming meetings. All times are IST.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Create Next Month's Meetings

    Creates all of next month's meetings for every chapter from each chapter's schedule. You see a preview before anything is created.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Meeting list

    Title, chapter, date, number of visitors registered, member reminder status and meeting status. Tap Edit to change a meeting.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Add a meeting

    For a one-off meeting: title, chapter, date and time, venue, address, map link, chief guest, agenda and description. Leave 'Send member reminder' ticked to email members 2 days before.

Website Admin · 18

Create next month's meetings

The preview screen for next month's meetings.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Preview

    Every meeting that will be created: chapter, date, time, title, venue and chief guest. Check it carefully.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Confirm

    Creates the meetings. Nothing is created until you tap this.

Website Admin · 19

Edit a meeting

Change a meeting's details and see who has registered to visit.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Member reminder

    Whether the 2-day reminder email is on, when it is scheduled and how many members will get it.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Meeting details

    Title, status (Scheduled, Completed or Cancelled), date and time, venue, chief guest, agenda, and whether visitors can register online and members get the reminder. Tap Save changes.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Registered visitors

    Everyone who registered to visit this meeting.

Website Admin · 20

Visitors

Everyone who registered online to visit a chapter meeting. Follow them up here and send the strong ones to Apply.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Visitor list

    Name, category, chapter, the meeting they registered for, status and date.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: View

    Opens the visitor's details.

Website Admin · 21

Visitor — follow up

One visitor's details and follow-up.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Visitor details

    Contact details, company, category, purpose of visit and who invited them.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Notes

    Follow-up notes for the team.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Status

    Registered, Attended, Follow-up required, Interested in membership, Application submitted, Converted or Not interested.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Ready to apply?

    Opens the public Apply page so the visitor can apply for membership.

Website Admin · 22

Website Content

Text blocks the public website and the portals read: about text, contact details, fees, footer, payment details, roster text and stats. Leave a box empty to use the site's default.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: About and Contact

    The About page introduction and the email, office address and phone shown on the site.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Fees

    Annual membership fee, monthly meeting charge and visitor fees. Each box has its own Save button.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Payment details

    The QR code images and bank details members see on their Payments page.

Website Admin · 23

Blog

Website blog posts, including articles members submit from the member portal.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Start a draft

    Type a title, pick a category and author, then tap Start draft to open the editor.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Posts

    All posts with status (draft or published) and member submissions waiting for approval or rejection.

Website Admin · 24

Testimonials

Testimonials shown on the website.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Testimonial list

    Name, type, status and whether it is featured.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Add a testimonial

    Link it to a member (fills in name and company), choose a type, write the testimonial, add a photo or video and tick the consent box. Publishes immediately.

Website Admin · 25

Chief Guests

Chief guests who have visited chapters. Published ones appear in the homepage carousel, and you can pick them on meetings and invitation posters.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Chief guest list

    Name, company, chapter, date visited, display order and whether shown on the homepage.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Add a chief guest

    Name, organisation, designation, chapter, date, display order, photo and a short description.

Website Admin · 26

FAQs

Questions and answers on the website's FAQ page.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Add an FAQ

    Type the question and answer, then tap Add FAQ.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Existing FAQs

    Each FAQ can be edited in place and saved, hidden from the website, or deleted.

Website Admin · 27

Feedback

Feedback submitted from the website. Only the Super Admin can see this unless they grant access.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Feedback list

    When it was sent, type, who sent it, chapter or meeting, rating and message.

Website Admin · 28

Vendor Contact Requests

When a website visitor taps 'View contact details' on a member's profile, they leave their own details first. Those requests are listed here.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Filter

    By chapter, name, or date range.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Requests

    When, who asked, which member they viewed and what they need.

Website Admin · 29

Users (admin accounts)

Admin accounts only. Member portal logins are managed from Member Login Credentials and each member's page.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Admin list

    Name, email, role, status and last login. Suspend blocks an admin from signing in; you can change their role here too.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Create admin user

    Name, email, a temporary password and the role (Central Admin, Chapter Admin, Accounts, Website, Membership or Marketing Admin). Chapter Admins are tied to one chapter.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Download list

    A PDF of all admin accounts.

Website Admin · 30

Roles & Permissions

Decide what each role can do. Tick a box to give a permission, untick to remove it. Changes apply the next time each user signs in.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Roles

    One column per role: Accounts Department, Central Admin, Chapter Admin, Marketing Admin, Member, Membership Admin, Super Admin and Website Admin.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Permissions

    One row per permission. A dark box means that role has it.

Good to know
  • Super Admin's permissions are fixed so nobody can lock themselves out.
  • Role and permission changes may ask you to re-enter your password if you signed in more than 15 minutes ago.
Website Admin · 31

Settings

Super Admin only.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Feedback email recipients

    Who gets an email when feedback is submitted. Switch people on or off, or add an email address.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: First-login email check

    When on, members confirm their email with a 6-digit code during activation. Turn off only if email delivery isn't working.

Website Admin · 32

Activity Log

A complete history of sign-ins and admin actions: who did what, and when.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Log

    When, who, the action and what it affected.

Website Admin · 33

Change password

Opened from 'Change password' at the bottom of the sidebar.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Change password

    Type your current password, then your new password twice.

Part 3

Member Performance Admin

Running meetings and tracking members: roster sheets, check-in QR codes, attendance, invitation posters, visitors, payments and points.

Member Performance Admin · 34

Roster Sheets

Build the printable roster sheet for a chapter meeting: members, scores and ranking, open categories and notes.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Pick a chapter

    Choose the chapter and tap Continue, then pick the meeting.

Member Performance Admin · 35

Roster Sheets — build the roster

The roster builder works in steps, shown at the top.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Steps

    Manage members and scores, select open categories, configure notes, review and save, then download the PDF.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Chief guest

    Tick a chief guest to feature them on the cover.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Teams

    Founding team, leadership and coordinators come from the chapter's settings page.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Scores and ranking

    Scores start from each member's overall points. Change a score, then Re-rank. Moving a member with the arrows or changing a serial number switches to manual order.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Members

    Serial number, member details, category, Give & Ask notes, overall points and the score printed on the roster.

  6. 6
    Marker 6: Next

    Move to the next step.

Member Performance Admin · 36

QR Codes — pick a meeting

Members check in to meetings by scanning a QR code. First pick the chapter, then the meeting.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Generate QR

    Creates a secure check-in QR for a meeting that doesn't have one yet.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: View QR

    Opens a meeting's existing QR. Reopening never makes a new one.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Create a new meeting

    If the meeting isn't listed yet.

Member Performance Admin · 37

QR Codes — meeting QR and check-in window

Show this QR at the venue. Members scan it, sign in and check themselves in.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: The QR code

    Download it, Print it, or open the registration link to test it.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Open / Close registration

    Members can check in only while registration is Open. Close it when check-in time is over.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Check-in window

    Optional. Set the time check-in opens and closes (IST), then Save window. This also applies to the visitor QR.

Member Performance Admin · 38

Attendance — one meeting

Pick a chapter and meeting on the Attendance page to see who was there.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Summary

    Members expected, present, absent and the attendance percentage.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Member attendance

    Each member's status and check-in time.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Correct

    Fix a mistake, for example mark someone Present who couldn't scan. You must give a reason; it is logged.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Close meeting

    After the meeting, marks everyone who didn't check in as Absent.

Good to know
  • The Attendance page also has Export Excel and a Member-wise view to see one member's attendance across meetings.
Member Performance Admin · 39

Meeting Invitations

Make a shareable invitation poster for a meeting. Pick the chapter and meeting; the details fill in from the meeting.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Header

    The top line and headline of the poster.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Chief guests

    Add up to 3 chief guests from the list or manually. Leave empty to drop that section.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Meeting details

    Date, time, venue, address and website. Edit if needed.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: QR code

    Choose what the poster's QR opens: venue location, visitor registration or meeting payment.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: Live preview

    Updates as you type. 'Download Invitation (PNG)' saves a 1350 × 2400 image ready for WhatsApp.

  6. 6
    Marker 6: Save Invitation

    Saves your changes so you can come back to it.

Member Performance Admin · 40

Visitors QR

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Visitor QR

    Download, print or open the link.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Open / Close

    Visitors can check in only while this is Open.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: Check-in window

    Shared with the member QR for this meeting.

Member Performance Admin · 41

Visitors Attendance

Who visited a meeting, who invited them and where they heard about BWF.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Filters

    Search by visitor, category, inviting member or source (invited by a member, Instagram, Facebook, Google and so on).

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Visitor attendance

    Each visitor with phone, company, category, inviter or source, status and check-in time. Use Correct to fix a status.

Member Performance Admin · 42

Payments — review and approve

Members' payment submissions: monthly meeting fees (at check-in or from the member portal) and annual membership. A payment counts only after it is approved.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Totals

    Total submitted, approved, pending and rejected amounts.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Filters

    Filter by chapter, meeting, member, type (meeting fee or membership), status and date. Tap Apply.

  3. 3
    Marker 3: A payment

    Who paid, for which months, amount, payment date, and 'View proof' to open the screenshot.

  4. 4
    Marker 4: Approve / Reject / Request clarification

    Approve once the money is confirmed in the bank. Reject or ask the member for clarification if something doesn't match; the member sees the status in their portal.

  5. 5
    Marker 5: More

    Payment links to share with members, Excel export, and the member-wise view.

Member Performance Admin · 43

Payment Links

Share a link that takes members straight to the right payment page.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Meeting payment link

    Edit the message, then Copy link, Share on WhatsApp, Copy message + link, or Download QR.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Membership link

    The same for the yearly membership payment.

Member Performance Admin · 44

Visitors Payment

Meeting fees paid by visitors. Review and approve them the same way as member payments.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Filters

    Filter by chapter, meeting, visitor and status. Payments are listed below with Approve, Reject and Request clarification.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Save

    Each row has its own Save button. Members' scores update straight away.

Member Performance Admin · 46

  1. 1

  2. 2

    The points leaderboard, or every referral, thank you slip, one-to-one, power date, conclave, consumer, chief guest and induction.

  3. 3

Member Performance Admin · 47

BWF App Activity — one member

One member's full activity.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: Points breakdown

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Activity lists

Marketing Admin · 49

Content Calendar

Plan content and see scheduled posts by date. Times are IST.

  1. 1
    Marker 1: View

    Switch between Month, Week and Day.

  2. 2
    Marker 2: Move between dates

Help

Common questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most. Each one links to the page that shows it.

How do I see who changed something?

Activity Log lists every sign-in and admin action with who did it and when. See: Activity Log →

How do I mark absentees after a meeting?

Open the meeting on Attendance and tap Close Meeting & Mark Absentees. Everyone who didn't check in is marked Absent. See: Attendance — one meeting →

Members can't check in to a meeting.

Open QR Codes for that meeting and make sure Registration is Open. If a check-in window is set, members can check in only inside it. See: QR Codes — meeting QR and check-in window →

I can't add a member because the category is taken.

Only one active member can hold a category in each chapter. Deactivate the current holder first, or choose another category. See: Members — list →

A member lost their temporary password.

Tick them on Member Login Credentials and use Reset to temporary password. This works only for members who haven't activated yet. See: Member Login Credentials →

An activated member forgot their password.

They can reset it themselves with Forgot password? on the member sign-in page. See: Member Login Credentials →

Can I see a member's temporary password again later?

No. Passwords are shown once, right after you generate them. Download the PDF or Excel straight away; later you can only reset. See: Member Login Credentials — after generating →

How do I turn an application into a member?

Open the application and, once the membership payment is confirmed, use Create member account. See: Application — review and convert →

How do I approve or reject a payment?

Open Payments (or All Payments in Accounts) and use Approve, Reject or Request Clarification on the pending payment. See: Payments — review and approve →

Why can't I approve payments?

Approving needs the payment approval permission, held by Super Admin and Accounts Department by default. Central Admin can only view. See: Roles & Permissions →

How do I create next month's meetings for every chapter?

Use Create Next Month's Meetings on the Meetings page. It uses each chapter's recurring schedule, set on the chapter's settings page. See: Create next month's meetings →

A member was marked absent by mistake.

Open the meeting on Attendance and use Correct next to the member. You must give a reason. See: Attendance — one meeting →

Social media posts aren't publishing.

Posts publish only after the BWF accounts are connected on Connected Accounts. See: Connected Accounts →

Why can't I see some pages in the menu?

The menu shows only your current workspace and what your role allows. Switch workspace from the bottom of the sidebar, or ask the Super Admin for access. See: Choose a workspace →

It asks for my password again when I change roles.

Sensitive changes need a recent sign-in. If you signed in more than 15 minutes ago, enter your password again. See: Roles & Permissions →

How do I make a meeting invitation poster?

Open Meeting Invitations, pick the meeting, adjust the details and use Download Invitation (PNG). See: Meeting Invitations →

How do members get the meeting reminder email?

Leave Send member reminder ticked on the meeting. Members with an email address get it 2 days before. See: Edit a meeting →

Admin passwords and temporary member passwords shown here belong to the demo and do not work on the live site.
